About Me

Hello! I’m Anna Abrahamyan and welcome to my website! I use this space to share my work, projects, and ideas! I’m a Machine Learning & Data Science Specialist with a strong academic foundation, holding a BSc in Data Science and an MSc in Machine Learning & Data Mining. Over the past three years, I’ve worked across diverse domains including Natural Language Processing (NLP), recommendation systems, predictive modeling, optimization, and data-driven product development. I'm passionate about getting impactful insights from messy datasets. I enjoy building intelligent systems that not only perform well in theory but deliver measurable value in production environments.
